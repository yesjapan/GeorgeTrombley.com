Most Japanese textbooks make you choose between learning to speak and learning to
read. Book 1 refuses the choice. Hiragana arrives gradually — a handful of
characters per lesson, each one immediately used in real sentences — so the
writing system is absorbed rather than memorised in a block.

- Integrated workbook with answer key
- Over 800 new words and expressions
- Learn to read and write hiragana
- Example dialogues built from words you already know
- Culture notes on how Japanese is actually used
- Bilingual glossaries in kana and romaji
