Book 2 does for katakana what Book 1 did for hiragana, and the text stops using
romaji altogether. The grammar widens at the same time: past tense, adjective
conjugation, and the particle patterns that let you build sentences instead of
reciting them.

- New Japanese grammar concepts
- Over 800 new words and expressions
- Learn the katakana writing system
- Integrated workbook with answer key
