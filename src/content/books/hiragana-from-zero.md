The first Japanese writing system, pulled out of the main course and given a
book of its own. Every character gets stroke order, handwriting practice and
progressive review, so hiragana becomes something you read rather than something
you translate.

- Complete stroke-order guides for all 46 basic characters
- Handwriting practice grids
- Progressive review that reuses everything already learned
- Integrated workbook with answer key
