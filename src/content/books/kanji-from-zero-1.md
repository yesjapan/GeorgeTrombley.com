Kanji is usually taught in the order Japanese schoolchildren learn it, which is
a poor fit for an adult learner who wants to read a menu this year. This book
groups characters by usefulness and teaches the readings you will meet first,
with the same integrated-workbook structure as the rest of the series.

- Progressive introduction with immediate use in context
- Stroke order for every character
- On and kun readings taught as they are actually encountered
- Integrated workbook with answer key
