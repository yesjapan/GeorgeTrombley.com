Hangul is the most learnable writing system in the world, and it is often taught
as though that were the whole story: here are the letters, good luck. Knowing the
letters is not the same as reading, though, and the gap between the two is where
most beginners stall.

This book closes it. Every letter gets stroke order and handwriting practice, the
syllable blocks are built up rather than presented finished, and 250 real words
arrive as soon as there are enough letters to spell them — so you finish reading
hangul rather than decoding it.

- Every consonant and vowel, with stroke order
- Syllable-block construction taught step by step
- 250 vocabulary words using only the letters covered so far
- Integrated workbook with answer key
- Free downloadable audio
