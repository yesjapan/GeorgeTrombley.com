Katakana is the writing system learners most often skip and most often regret
skipping. It carries loanwords, emphasis, company names and menus. This book
gives it the same stroke-by-stroke, drill-until-automatic treatment as its
hiragana counterpart.

- Complete stroke-order guides
- Handwriting practice grids
- Loanword drills using vocabulary you already recognise
- Integrated workbook with answer key
